Enter average income and population, then choose Predict. The chart shows the estimated number of registered vehicles for each vehicle type. The model is the random forest from Assignment 2. It was trained on population aged above 15, in thousands, and mean household income in ringgit. It does not use year, population below 14, median income, vehicle price, or state. Blank values and text are rejected.
